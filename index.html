🔭 Selected Projects

University of Sydney Capstone · Ongoing

Scene- and Metadata-Aware Knowledge Graph Learning for Robust Vehicle Re-Identification

Role: Full-Stack Developer & Knowledge Graph Lead

Developing a knowledge-graph-enhanced vehicle Re-ID framework that connects vehicle images with camera, scene and attribute metadata. The heterogeneous graph uses attention-based message propagation to improve cross-camera retrieval under viewpoint, lighting and occlusion changes.

My contribution: heterogeneous graph design, node and relation definition, and graph-learning experiments. Evaluation uses VeRi-776 and CityFlow-ReID with Rank-1 accuracy, mAP and ablation studies.
