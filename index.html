Hi! I am Haoxuan Duan.

I am a Master of Cybersecurity candidate at the University of Sydney with a background in software engineering. My academic work spans quantum digital signatures, Web and Android application security, privacy and technology-risk governance.

I am particularly interested in research connecting technical security analysis with practical risk assessment, control design and responsible system development. I welcome academic discussions and potential collaboration in these areas.
